## Could not get (after ~25 min across Pexels, Pixabay, Unsplash, Mixkit, Coverr, Videvo)

1. **Maintenance in progress (videos, wanted 2–3; photos, wanted 3+)** — the free libraries simply do not have hobby/service-style footage of hands scraping glass, siphoning gravel, water changes, or test kits. Every "aquarium cleaning" hit was either a scuba diver in a public aquarium (Pexels 2558483, 9685662, 39457915, 1917831 — skipped per brief), a person with a visible face (8434705, 7509423), generic lab test tubes, or kitchen/car cleaning. The one clip saved (hand against tank glass) is an artistic substitute. Recommendation: shoot 3–4 phone clips on site (gloved hand + magnet cleaner, siphon into bucket, API test tube held to light) — 10 minutes of B-roll would fill this gap far better than stock.
2. **Luxury lobby/office built-in aquariums** — no free shot of a large custom built-in in a high-end lobby exists on these sites; everything is either a public aquarium with silhouetted crowds or a hotel lobby without a tank. Got one genuine modern living-room-with-aquarium (Unsplash) plus four clean home-office/living-room tanks. Unsplash ids L5krV5BkAuQ and CIt4K5nv6uQ ("large aquarium filled with colorful fish", Dmytro Bukhantsov) were not fetched because context could not be confirmed; worth a manual look.
3. **Photo categories are otherwise at or above target**: reef 14/6, freshwater 6/4 (+1 flagged), interiors 5/3, koi 3/2, locale 6/2, cutouts 10/6.
4. **Videos**: reef 6/3–4, freshwater 3/1–2, koi 2/1, bubbles 3/1–2. Note `bubbles-underwater-ascending-02.mp4` is 1280x720 despite Pexels labelling it 1080p; `maintenance-hand-submerged-in-tank-01.mp4` is 44 s (over the 30 s ask) and should be trimmed.
