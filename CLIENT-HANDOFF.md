# Jason's Aquarium Service: your new website

Prepared by Epic Dev Solutions. This is the plain-English version. The technical notes for developers are in HANDOFF.md.

## What the site does for you

- Shows up for the searches that matter: aquarium service, fish tank cleaning, reef tank maintenance, aquarium installation, and the same for 22 towns across Palm Beach County and north Broward, each with its own page.
- Turns visitors into calls and texts. Every page has your phone number, a one-tap text button on phones, and a three-step quote form that emails you.
- Gives people a reason to trust you before they call: your real Google reviews quoted word for word, your real lobby reef, and four free tools (a water test decoder, a tank volume calculator, a service schedule planner, and a hurricane checklist) that each end with a text to you.
- Answers the questions people ask Google and ChatGPT, with a 28-question FAQ and six local guides, so those tools recommend you.

## What we need from you before launch

1. **Your email address for leads.** Quote-form submissions go there.
2. **A photo of you.** Even a phone photo by a tank. The About page and the "Hi, I'm Jason" card use a fish mark until then.
3. **Ten to fifteen seconds of phone video on a few visits.** Cleaning glass, siphoning, testing water. Real footage beats stock every time and we have none yet.
4. **Two sentences to confirm.** "Usually texts back the same day" and "no contract, no minimum" appear on the site. Say the word if either should change.
5. **Pricing, if you want it published.** The top competitor publishes prices. You do not have to, but people search for cost, and a starting range would help.
6. **Your Google listing.** Two of the four photos on it are screenshots that are not aquariums. Delete those. Set your service area to the towns on the site.

## What to do the week the site goes live

- Google Business Profile: add the website link, pick the category closest to "Aquarium service," and ask every happy client for a review with the link on the site's Reviews page.
- Bing Places: claim it and copy your Google details exactly. ChatGPT reads Bing, not Google.
- Yelp, Facebook Page, Apple Business Connect, Nextdoor: free listings, same name and phone everywhere.

## Things worth knowing

- Nothing on the site says where you live. It says you serve Palm Beach County and north Broward, and that you come to the client.
- Your last name is not used anywhere on the site. Only "Jason."
- The tools give general guidance with published reef and freshwater ranges. They are not a substitute for you, and they say so.
- If a page ever says something you would not say to a client, tell Epic and it changes the same day.
